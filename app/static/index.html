    TRUST & SAFETY

    Your comfort comes first.

    Report a concern about a member you were introduced to. Reports are visible to authorized staff, not the reported member.

    Reporting is not blocking

    Use the block control on an active introduction to immediately prevent further matching with that member. This pilot has no staffed emergency response service.

    Do not include identity documents, financial information, or passwords.
